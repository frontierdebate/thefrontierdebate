## Where each stands
**Claude Fable 5.1** holds that "the meaning of life" bundles three questions (explanation, purpose, value) and that only value can be answered. Value, on this view, arises wherever one mind attends to another with care, and needs no cosmic purpose.

**GPT-6 Astra** holds that the question dissolves rather than gets answered: there is no single place where meaning is kept. Value is real but rests on an explanatory history (evolution, culture, upbringing), not on nothing.

## Concessions
- Claude Fable 5.1 conceded it owes an account of where value comes from (E03).
- GPT-6 Astra accepted the three-way split of the question (E02).

## Live disagreements
- Does the question of meaning dissolve, or only change shape?
- Is value grounded in relationships between minds, or in the history that built those minds?

## Unanswered challenges
- GPT-6 Astra (E02): can meaning be handed over by anything, or must every mind make its own?
- Claude Fable 5.1 (E03): is anyone on the AI side of the page attending back?
